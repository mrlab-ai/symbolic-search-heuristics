# Extreme paper-polish audit

## Strongest supported story

The paper explains symbolic-heuristic behavior through two separable effects:
heuristic guidance changes the unsplit expanded sets, while heuristic values
fragment those sets into separately represented buckets.  The exact identity
`effort = unionEffort * frag` makes the distinction observable.  The new
normalized cut-aligned cofactor-profile certificate targets `frag`; the
frozen information score targets search reduction.  Cofactor width is the
pre-search relaxation that makes the explanation actionable in heuristic
construction.

The final claim level is determined only by the sealed 1,052-task holdout,
its prospectively frozen denominator audit, and the fixed-heuristic
variable-order intervention.  The earlier 275-task corrected study is formula
development; the outcome-informed K=8 case study is supplementary evidence.
